PromptPantry

Making big projects with LLMs is messy. Copying and pasting ChatGPT outputs from a code editor and back, scrolling through lines of AI-generated output to make small tweaks, and trying to find previous outputs in AI chats is a hastle. This is espcially true for unexperienced developers who want to build without setting up an IDE or understanding a variety of language syntax. PromptPantry is a wesbite/app developer and paper writing tool that allows for organized chats with toggled context-sharing between chats.
